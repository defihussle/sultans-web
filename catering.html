Why us

Why .

Catering for your events shouldn’t be another source of strain; it should be simple, stunning, and sincerely welcoming to all.

We offer flexible catering packages to suit any occasion, including corporate events, client meetings, and team lunches in Toronto’s Financial District and surrounding areas.

Enjoy complimentary PATH delivery on all orders over $200.

How to book catering

Four simple steps.

  1. Browse the menu

    Pick a spread, platters or boxed meals for your guest count.

  2. Submit your order

    Call 647-929-3924 or send the request form below.

  3. Confirm details

    We send an invoice. Approve it and a payment link locks in the date.

  4. Eat & enjoy

    Prepared fresh for pickup or hot delivery at your time.
