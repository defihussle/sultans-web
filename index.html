How to book

Four simple steps.

No account, no online checkout, no back and forth. A real person handles your order from first message to delivery.

  1. Browse the menu

    Pick a buffet spread, platters or boxed meals based on your guest count.

  2. Submit your order

    Call 647-803-2323 or send the request form below.

  3. Confirm the details

    A rep sends an invoice to review. Approve it and a payment link locks in the date.

  4. Eat & enjoy

    Everything is prepared fresh for pickup or hot delivery at your scheduled time.
